# NonStop Financial — Curriculum Video Mapping

Working sheet for the ~40 videos Anna sent (`Platform Curriculum (1).zip`, ~90 GB).

**How this works:** the platform plays video from a **URL** (YouTube unlisted is the
chosen host). Upload each `.mp4` to YouTube (unlisted), then paste its link in the
**YouTube link** column. Once filled in, I wire every lesson in one pass.

- **Lesson ID** = the exact slot in the curriculum I'll attach the link to.
- Rows marked **⚠︎** need a decision (see _Needs a decision_ at the bottom).
- Folder numbers in the zip use the *old* scheme (Sales=6, Leads=10, Annuity=11);
  this sheet maps by **name** to the current curriculum.
